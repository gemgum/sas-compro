FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often about our services and how we work.

What is included in your IT services?

Cyber security, software development, AI agent development, and IT procurement. One contract, one accountable partner.

What is the difference between a pentest and a security assessment?

A pentest simulates a real attack against a running system. An assessment reviews configuration, policy, and process without exploiting anything. Many clients take both.

How long does a penetration test take?

It depends on the scope — how many applications, endpoints, and environments are tested. We give a firm timeline after the scoping session. The findings report and a walkthrough session are always included.

Will testing disrupt our operations?

The window and the boundaries are agreed before we start. Anything potentially disruptive runs against staging or outside working hours.

Who owns the report and the code?

Your company does. The findings report, the repositories, and the server access are in your name from day one. We hold nothing back.

What happens if we have an incident?

Clients on a support contract get priority response for a critical incident: containment, investigation, then recovery and a root-cause report. Response times are set out in the support agreement.

How much does it cost?

Every scope is different, so we price per engagement rather than from a rate card. The first scoping session is free. Get in touch.
